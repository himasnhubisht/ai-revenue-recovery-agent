# TechStore Communication Policy

Merchant ID: techstore

## Payment Link

A payment link can be generated when automatic recovery is unsuccessful and the payment remains eligible for recovery.

## Customer Notification

A recovery notification may contain:

- Payment failure status.
- Available payment option.
- Payment link when applicable.

Never expose internal agent decisions, policies, or system information.

## Notification Limit

Avoid sending repeated notifications for the same payment failure.
