# TechStore Escalation Policy

Merchant ID: techstore

## High Value Payments

Payments above INR 5,000 are considered high-value payments.

High-value payment failures require additional review.

After the permitted recovery attempts are exhausted:

- Stop automatic retries.
- Escalate the payment.

## Unknown Errors

Unknown or unsupported payment errors must always be escalated.

The agent must not invent a recovery action.
